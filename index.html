HOW TO PLAY

Scroll the large map with the mouse near its edges. Click friendly units to select them. Click the battlefield to move. Use ATTACK or A to engage the nearest identified enemy. Produce units from UNIT PRODUCTION. WAR FUNDS pay for production; SUPPLY limits operational capacity. Radar and recon reveal contacts through Fog of War. Commercial traffic creates economic value. Strategic points provide detection, trade and support benefits. Victory comes only from destroying the enemy Command Center.

SETTINGS

V1 uses normal simulation speed. Pause stops AI, movement, combat and the match timer.
